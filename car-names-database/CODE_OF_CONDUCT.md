# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为促进开放友好的环境,我们作为贡献者与维护者承诺:**无论年龄、体型、残障、种族、民族特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状况、国籍、个人外貌、宗教或性取向如何,参与本项目都不会受到骚扰**。

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括:

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- 使用友好与包容的语言 / Using welcoming and inclusive language
- 尊重不同的观点与经验 / Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- 优雅地接受建设性批评 / Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- 关注对社区最有利的事 / Focusing on what is best for the community
- 对其他成员展现同理心 / Showing empathy towards other community members

不可接受的行为包括:

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- 使用性别化语言或意象,以及不受欢迎的性关注 / Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论,以及人身或政治攻击 / Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下的骚扰 / Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息(如住址、邮箱)/ Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- 在专业环境中可合理认为不适当的其他行为 / Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## 我们的责任 / Our Responsibilities

项目维护者负责澄清可接受行为的标准,并对任何不可接受的行为采取恰当、公平的纠正措施。

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本行为准则适用于项目空间内,以及代表本项目或社区时(如作为官方社交媒体账号或参与线上/线下活动)的公共空间。

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## 执行 / Enforcement

如有虐待、骚扰或其他不可接受行为,可通过 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 中的联系方式向维护团队报告。维护团队将审阅并调查所有投诉,并作出其认为必要且适当的回应。维护者有义务对事件举报人保密。

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainer team via the channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer team will review and investigate all complaints, and will respond in a way it deems necessary and appropriate. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
